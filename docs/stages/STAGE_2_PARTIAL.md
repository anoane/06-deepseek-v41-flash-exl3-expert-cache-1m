## What the partial stage 2 says (to be confirmed by second processes and stage 3)

1. **Allocator (2.4).** Expandable segments instead of cudaMallocAsync is the best bit-exact change so far: +5.5% decode
   on C12 in two processes, identical tokens. The CMP-side layer time drops (H7: CMP blocks 0.86-0.87 ms mean instead
   of 0.96) and the CMP keeps 1.3 GB free instead of 0.8 GB at 1M.
2. **Pipeline and chunking (2.1).** The pipeline sub-chunk is bounded by CMP memory at 1M: at split 12, 4096 fails
   under cudaMallocAsync, 3072 fails 1 job in 15, 2560 and 2048 fit; under expandable segments 4096 fits
   (`C12-ES-P4`: +37% prefill, the same tokens as plain C12). The generator must hold several sub-chunks per call:
   2 per call gained nothing (`C11-P8`), 8 per call gained 6-7% over 4 (`C11-G32`), and the unpatched tabby shape (2048-row
   sub-chunks, two per 4096-token call, `C11-T`) lost 36%. At split 10, 8192-row chunks gave +11% over 4096.
3. **BF16 crossing (2.2).** +12% prefill on plain C12, +16-17% on pipelined C11, +14-17% on C10 at 8192; decode
   unchanged. It changes numerics (lossy class) and its 64K quality lines did not run.
4. **Precise numerics (2.8).** +6.1% decode on C12 in one short process (+2.7% on L0 in stage 0), at a known quality
   cost against the default's NLL. Not adopted; a decision for later.
5. **Tier policy (2.5).** `admit=always` barely moves the average (+0.7%) but shrinks the topic-switch collapse (23%
   vs 37%) and nearly halves its recovery (129 vs 225 tokens); `lfu` is slightly worse; a heat file makes the first
   request after a load faster (+3.5% decode, +12.9% prefill) and changes nothing after that.
6. **Pool room (2.4).** Single staging buys 384 slots and +1.2% decode for -6.5% prefill; headroom cuts buy 161-242
   slots for nothing measurable. The pool is not the bottleneck at these hit rates.
7. **Engram RAM (2.3).** A 30-40 GiB engram row cache is within noise (+1.4% prefill at best); the page cache already
   serves the hot rows. On `5e55c062`, `ram ngram=auto` over-sizes the row cache (96 GiB); a Python-only fix exists and
   is not on the published branch yet.
8. **Prefill rows (2.7).** Routed mode up to 1,024 rows makes chat follow-ups 8% faster to first token, decode
   unchanged: a stage-3 candidate.
9. **Config a (2.9).** a's prefill scales with the chunk (2,026-2,154 tok/s at 16K) because it is staging-bound, but it
   keeps reading experts from the SSD and loses 1-3% decode. It stays the reference, not a candidate.
